ADVISORY

ADV 2015-001

To all concerned offices and members of the Bids and Awards Committee (BAC):

In accordance with the applicable procurement guidelines and the standards set forth for transparent and accountable public bidding, all BAC members and concerned offices are hereby directed to strictly observe the procedures below in the conduct of procurement activities.

  1. Ensure that the approved Purchase Request, source of funds, and supporting documents are complete before the procurement process begins.
  2. Prepare and review the Request for Quotation or bidding documents carefully, and certify that the procurement mode selected is appropriate for the proposed transaction.
  3. Conduct the opening of bids, evaluation, and post-qualification in a fair, orderly, and documented manner, with all findings properly recorded in the BAC minutes.
  4. Submit the recommendation for award, the abstract of bids, and all related documentary requirements to the appropriate approving authority without delay.

Compliance with these procedures is essential to maintain the integrity of the procurement process and to uphold the principles of efficiency, accountability, and public trust.

This Advisory shall take effect immediately and shall be observed accordingly.
