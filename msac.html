Municipal Services Advisory Committee

MSAC

The Municipal Services Advisory Committee (MSAC) serves as an advisory body to ensure effective and efficient delivery of municipal services.

About MSAC

The Municipal Services Advisory Committee (MSAC) is a mechanism established to strengthen local governance by providing advisory support to municipal governments. It aims to enhance service delivery through coordinated efforts between national agencies and local government units.

Key Functions

📋

Advisory Support

Provide technical advice and recommendations to municipal LGUs on improving service delivery and governance systems.

🤝

Coordination

Facilitate coordination between national government agencies, provincial offices, and municipal LGUs for aligned program implementation.

📊

Monitoring & Evaluation

Monitor the implementation of municipal programs and projects, and evaluate their impact on local communities.

📚

Capacity Building

Support capacity development initiatives for municipal officials and staff to enhance their technical and administrative capabilities.

Scope of Services

The MSAC covers advisory services across the following areas:

  • Local Governance Performance — Advisory on LGPMS, SGLG compliance, and governance performance improvement.
  • Fiscal Administration — Guidance on local fiscal management, budgeting, and resource mobilization.
  • Social Services — Advisory on the delivery of health, education, social welfare, and other basic services.
  • Infrastructure & Development — Technical assistance on local planning, infrastructure projects, and economic development.
