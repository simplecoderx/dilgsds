GAD PLANS AND REPORTS

This section contains official GAD documents prepared and submitted in compliance with national requirements:

GAD Plan and Budget (GPB)

The GPB outlines the agency's gender issues, objectives, programs, activities, and corresponding budget allocations for each fiscal year.

GAD Accomplishment Report (GAR)

The GAR presents the results and accomplishments of implemented GAD programs and activities, including outcomes and utilization of funds.

GAD Audit and Monitoring Reports

These reports reflect the findings, recommendations, and compliance status based on GAD audits and evaluations.
