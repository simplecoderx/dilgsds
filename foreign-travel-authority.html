Issuance of Certificate for Foreign Travel Authority of Local Government Officials and Employees

This service covers the issuance of a certificate authorizing local government officials and employees to travel abroad for official business, study or training programs, and other lawful purposes in accordance with government rules and procedures.

Office or Division Bureau of Local Government Supervision (BLGS); DILG Regional Offices; DILG Field Offices
Classification Complex Transaction
Type of Transaction G2C – Government to Citizens
Who May Avail Local Government Employees and Local Government Elective Officials

Checklist of Requirements

No. Requirement Where to Secure
1
  1. For Study or Training Trips
    1. Letter of request from the Local Chief Executive or Head of Office.
    2. Approved travel itinerary showing dates, destination, purpose, and duration.
    3. Copy of acceptance, endorsement, or invitation from the host institution or training provider.
    4. Program of activities or study plan, if applicable.
    5. Proof of funding support or sponsorship, if any.
  2. For Non-Study Trips
    1. Letter-request addressed to the approving authority indicating the official purpose of travel.
    2. Approved itinerary, mission order, or office travel order.
    3. Certification of official business from the LGU or office.
    4. Supporting documents such as invitations, conference schedules, or meeting invitations.
  • Office of the Local Chief Executive
  • Administrative or Human Resource Office
  • Training or Scholarship Coordinator
  • Host Institution or Conference Organizer
2
  1. For Personal Trips of Governors, Mayors of Highly Urbanized Cities, and Independent Component Cities
    1. Formal written request for travel authority.
    2. Travel itinerary, including dates, destination, and purpose of travel.
    3. Affidavit or sworn statement confirming that the trip is personal and not official in nature.
    4. Proof of financial capacity or travel arrangements, where required.
  • Office of the Governor or Mayor
  • Local Executive Office
  • Legal or Administrative Office
3
  1. Supporting government clearances
    1. Copy of passport or identification document, where required.
    2. Visa or entry document, if already secured.
    3. Clearance from the concerned department or office, if applicable.
  • Passport Office or Travel Agency
  • Embassy or Consular Office
  • LGU Administration Office

Where to Secure

No. Service / Item Where to Secure
1 Letter of request, itinerary, and official travel purpose Office of the Local Chief Executive / Head of Office / Administrative Office
2 Training, study, or conference invitation and program details Host Institution, Training Provider, Conference Secretariat, or Sponsoring Agency
3 Travel documents and identification Passport Office, Embassy / Consular Office, or Travel Agency
4 Clearance and approval for travel authority Bureau of Local Government Supervision (BLGS), DILG Regional Office, or DILG Field Office
📌 Note: For clients from NCR and HUCs and ICCs in other regions, please note that agency actions to be done at the "Provincial Office" shall be assumed by the concerned Regional Office (as applicable).
