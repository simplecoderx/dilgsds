Office or Division DILG Regional Offices; DILG Field Offices
Classification Simple Transaction
Type of Transaction G2C – Government to Citizens
Who May Avail
  • All Sanggunian Members (PCM) who served an aggregate of six (6) years, completed at least 72 academic units leading to a bachelor's degree (first-level eligibility), or an aggregate of nine (9) years with a completed baccalaureate degree (second-level eligibility), subject to applicable eligibility requirements.
  • All Barangay Elected Officials who completed one full term of service.
  • All Barangay Secretaries and Treasurers who served an aggregate of three (3) years in the same position.
  • All Barangay Secretaries and Treasurers who rendered the required continuous years of satisfactory service corresponding to the term of the appointing Punong Barangay, including services rendered under previous appointments, as applicable.
