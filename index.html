DILG Statement on the BJMP's Custody of Senator Rodante Dizon Marcoleta

July 15, 2026

The Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), through the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), confirms the commitment of Senator Rodante Dizon Marcoleta to the Quezon City Jail–Male Dormitory at 10:49 AM on July 15, 2026, pursuant to a Commitment Order issued by the Sandiganbayan Third Division in connection with a plunder case.

The DILG assures the public that the BJMP will continue to uphold its mandate with professionalism, impartiality, and strict adherence to established rules and procedures. No person deprived of liberty under the Bureau's custody shall be accorded special treatment.

      Vision

      The Province of Surigao del Sur envisions a safe, progressive, and inclusive community where every citizen enjoys quality public services, effective governance, and sustainable local development.

      Mission

      • Advance transparent and accountable governance across all local government units.
      • Empower communities through strengthened public participation and responsive service delivery.
      • Support inclusive economic growth, peace, and resilience for all barangays.

      Goals

      • Protect and enhance local governance systems through capacity-building and digital adoption.
      • Increase access to quality social services for vulnerable communities.
      • Promote stronger partnerships with national agencies, LGUs, and civil society.
      • Deliver timely information, programs, and technical assistance for sustainable development.
