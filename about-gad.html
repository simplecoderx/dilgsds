About GAD

The Gender and Development (GAD) program is a government strategy that promotes gender equality and ensures that the needs and concerns of women, men, and gender-diverse individuals are addressed in development planning, budgeting, implementation, and monitoring.

Through gender-responsive governance, GAD aims to eliminate discrimination, reduce gender gaps, and empower all sectors of society to actively participate in sustainable development.
